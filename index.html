Home Ballpark Spotlight

Rogers Centre: 59 Sections, No Runaway Winner

Rogers Centre logged 222 tracked home runs in 2025 across 59 named sections, the most sections of any park in this analysis, and none of them dominates. The top spot, general admission standing room out in centre field, accounts for just 10 of those 222 balls, a 4.5% share. Search "Blue Jays" above to load the full breakdown, including the suite tiers in right field where several sections sit almost directly on top of one another.

Source: Canada Sports Betting analysis of 2025 MLB Statcast home run tracking data and public seating chart data for 28 MLB ballparks.
